Code of Conduct

Integrity, Respect, and the Spirit of the Game.

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Core Philosophy

Elite Football Hub is built on the belief that local football is a tool for community growth. Every manager, player, and official is expected to uphold the highest standards of sportsmanship both on the digital platform and on the physical pitch.

Professionalism

Managers must ensure all player data is accurate. Impersonation or falsifying team records will result in immediate permanent suspension from the hub.

Fair Competition

Match requests must be honored once accepted. Canceling games without a 24-hour notice affects the ranking and reputation of both teams.

Zero Tolerance: Bullying

Any form of harassment, hate speech, or physical threats—whether in person or via our reporting system—is strictly prohibited.

Dispute Resolution

Conflicts regarding match outcomes must be reported via the Official Report Form with photo/video evidence where possible.

2. Enforcement

The Elite Football Hub officials reserve the right to:

  • Issue formal warnings to team managers.
  • Temporary freeze on match-requesting privileges.
  • Permanent removal of team and player profiles from the directory.

By using this platform, you agree to abide by these terms.
